# Code of Conduct

We are committed to an inclusive, respectful environment for contributors and service users. Do not engage in harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, or disclosure of client information. Offer constructive criticism and respect lived experience. Project maintainers may moderate contributions and participation that undermine safety or inclusion. Raise concerns privately with maintainers rather than posting sensitive details publicly.

This initial document will be reviewed with early community contributors before adopting a formal enforcement and appeal process.
