# SOAR Desk Project Charter

## Mission
Equip nonprofit disability advocates with free, auditable, secure, accessible tools for organizing SSI/SSDI application assistance, especially for people experiencing homelessness or barriers to care.

## Commitments
- No fees for essential self-hosted or desktop software features.
- Data ownership and agency control; no telemetry or external AI calls by default.
- Plain-language and accessible interfaces.
- Transparent workflows, cited rules, and human review of consequential decisions.
- No automated disability determinations or blanket diagnosis-based exclusions.
- Open participation from practitioners, security engineers, nonprofits, and people with lived experience.

## Scope
Initially: adult SSI/SSDI referrals, intake, records, evidence, applications, deadlines, and outcomes. Children's SSI, state-specific procedures, and appeals extensions require separately reviewed modules.

## Boundaries
This project is not a federal agency system or legal/medical advice service. It is not affiliated with SSA, SAMHSA, or PRA. Compliance and clinical accuracy require continuing review by competent professionals. No real case data before organizational authorization and security validation.

## Governance, initial phase
Maintainer-led with public RFCs, documented decisions, issue templates, and code review. A multi-stakeholder governance model should be adopted before organizational deployment or significant external contributions. See CONTRIBUTING.md.
